Open Secret Manager

With this on, the extension can read and change every https page you open, and puts a small script into each of them. A page's call for a passkey then goes to Open Secret Manager, which asks you what to do; one the app does not answer goes to the browser's own passkeys. A card's number in another site's frame, as a payment provider's, can then be filled when you press the button.

With it off, the extension reaches only the page you press its button on, and runs nothing in pages otherwise.
